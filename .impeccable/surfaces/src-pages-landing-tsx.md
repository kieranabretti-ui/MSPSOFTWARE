# Surface: the Headroom landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: an MSP owner who suspects they give work away. They must believe the product understands their business and act: run the demo or start an audit. Proof on hand: the real app and the demo MSP's real figures only; no customers, testimonials or integrations exist, so none appear.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the audit. It asks "How much money is your MSP giving away?" and the real product answers in the first viewport: the figure counts up, the GapBar opens, and findings land in a ledger as if the analysis just ran on the demo MSP. It refuses the centred headline over a gradient and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: The workspace's world at marketing scale: ink canvas, graphite frames with hairlines, bone display type up to 80px, tabular numerals as the hero material, lime only on found money and the primary action.

STORY: The visitor recognises their own leaks with concrete pounds, understands Upload, Analyse, Recover, sees the actual product and a client example, and runs the demo or starts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar with the logo, section links, Sign in and "Find My Lost Revenue". The question set large across the top left, supporting copy and both CTAs under it. Below and to the right, an audit panel built from the real components: £4,281 counting up, the GapBar, opportunities, clients at risk, £356 MRR, and the first findings arriving in a ledger list, labelled as the demo MSP.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction, no seed roll. Signature interaction: the audit run, an orchestrated count-up and ledger reveal that respects reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
