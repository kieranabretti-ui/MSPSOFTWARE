# Surface: the Headroom workspace (/app and every page under it)

Mode: Operate. Audience: MSP owners and commercial leads reviewing where revenue leaks and what to do next. Task: read the money first, then the critical findings, then client risk, then actions, then supporting data. Constraints: all existing routes, data, behaviour, labels and test ids stay; figures are potential, never promised.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The workspace is a ledger of found money. Graphite surfaces and bone type carry the data; lime appears only where money is found or recoverable and on the one money action per screen. It refuses the grey admin panel with coloured pills on every row.

OWN-WORLD: Ink canvas #0a0b0d, graphite surfaces stepped in small lightness steps, hairline borders, bone text, one lime accent #c4f25b, Host Grotesk with tabular figures, 8px controls and 12px cards, severity as a label plus a bar count, status colours calm and small.

STORY: The owner sees how much is leaking and how much of it recurs, knows which findings and clients to act on first, opens the evidence, and turns it into an action or a report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail on the canvas with the mark, wordmark and seven sections. Page title "Revenue Protection". A hero panel with £4,281 at data-xl, its label, and the GapBar of billed against unbilled for the period; beside it £356 a month in lime and £4,272 annualised. Critical findings and client risk begin inside the first desktop viewport. "View report" sits top right.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (Kieran's brand brief pins dark plus lime), so no seed roll. Signature move: the GapBar, billed in neutral and the unbilled gap in lime, used in the hero, client rows and client pages.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
