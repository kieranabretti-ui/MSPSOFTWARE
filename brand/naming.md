# Naming: from MSP Leak to Headroom

Decided 6 October 2026. **The product is renamed Headroom.** MSP Leak was a working name.

## The recommendation

**Headroom.** Tagline: *Find the work your MSP is doing for free.*

Headroom is the space between where you are and your limit. MSP owners already use the word twice a week: headroom on a server, a circuit or a licence pool, and headroom in a margin or a budget. The product's whole job is to show how much room each client has to be more profitable, so the name describes the outcome rather than the problem.

- **Why it is strongest.** It scored highest of 48 names (6.83 weighted, three independent judges), it is the only top name with no clash in MSP tooling, billing or finance software (web research found only small unrelated uses), and it scored highest of all on ability to expand (8.7 of 10).
- **What it could become.** "Leak detection" is one feature of Headroom. Pricing, contract sizing, billing reconciliation, forecasting and automated change orders are all "finding headroom". The name holds a company, not a feature: Headroom, Headroom Pricing, Headroom Forecast.
- **How it sounds.** HED-room. Two syllables, one way to say it, one way to spell it. It sounds calm and confident in a sentence: "We ran it through Headroom", "Headroom found £4,281".
- **How it looks.** Eight letters with a strong rhythm of ascenders (H, d) and round letters (e, a, o, o, m). It sets well as a single word in a semibold grotesk and leaves the mark to carry the idea: a level with room above it.
- **Why an MSP owner would trust it.** It is a word they already use about capacity and money, it does not sound like a cybersecurity vendor (MSP Leak's "leak" reads as a data breach to this audience), and it does not promise anything it cannot prove.
- **Tagline.** "Find the work your MSP is doing for free." stays. It is specific, owner-voiced and provocative, and it pairs with a calm name.

**Watch-outs.** Every obvious domain is registered (headroom.com since 1997, plus .co, .io, .ai, .app, .uk and the get/use/try/hq .com forms). headroommsp.com is unregistered at the .com registry (Verisign lookup, 6 October 2026), so it is a usable launch domain while headroom.com is approached. "Headroom" is a common word, so search needs the modifier ("Headroom for MSPs") and a UK trademark search in classes 9, 35, 36 and 42 is needed before launch.

## The alternatives

**#2 Margent.** An archaic English word for margin (Shakespeare uses it), with "argent", silver, inside it. The most ownable name on the list: no company found using it, low search noise, and getmargent.com is unregistered at the .com registry. It could become a full commercial-intelligence brand. Spoken, it is MAR-jent, but some people will say a hard g and some will read it as a typo of "margin". Visually it is compact and classical. An MSP owner would trust it once they hear the story; on first sight it needs explaining. Tagline: *The margin in every contract.*

**#3 TrueUp.** From the true-up: the reconciliation that brings billing back in line with reality, which every MSP knows from Microsoft licensing. It scored 9.0 of 10 on MSP relevance, the highest of any name; an owner gets it instantly. It could become the reconciliation and change-order brand. Spoken, TROO-up, unambiguous; written, the capitalisation wobbles (TrueUp, True-Up, Trueup). Its limits are that "true-up" is a generic billing term (weak trademark), trueup.io (a tech jobs tracker) owns the search results, and it sounds like a feature rather than a company. trytrueup.com is unregistered at the .com registry. Tagline: *True up every client.*

## How the decision was made

1. **Generation.** Four namers worked independently across the seven territories in the brief (direct and commercial, abstract SaaS, MSP-specific, financial intelligence, detection and visibility, premium and enterprise, invented), producing 47 names. MSP Leak was scored alongside them as the baseline.
2. **Domains.** Every name was checked across .com, .co, .io, .ai, .app, .uk and .co.uk, plus get-, use-, try- and -hq .com, using the public DNS. A name with nameservers is registered. A name with none is only *probably* free, so it is marked "availability requires verification" unless the registry itself confirmed it.
3. **Conflicts.** Four researchers searched the web for every name, looking for companies, products and trademarks in software, IT services, MSP tooling, fintech, billing and accounting.
4. **Scoring.** Three judges scored every name 1 to 10 on the eleven criteria in the brief: a 25-person UK MSP owner, a brand strategist, and a B2B SaaS investor. The weighted total doubles memorability, commercial feel and expandability, and gives professionalism 1.5x, because the brief names memorability, commercial credibility and room to build a large brand as the criteria that matter most.
5. **Judgement.** The weighted score ranks the names, but a direct conflict in our own category rules a name out whatever it scores.

## What each name means, and what the research found

**Headroom** (HED-room). The space between where you are and your limit. In finance it means margin or covenant headroom. In IT it means spare capacity on a server, circuit or licence pool. Conflicts: Headroom (startup on Dealroom and CB Insights) (Venture-backed startup, possibly health-related); Headroom Assistance (Remote executive-assistant software (G2 seller)); Helix HeadRoom (Software product); Headroom (application on Hostinger) (Web application).

**Margent** (MAR-jent). An archaic English word for "margin", used by Shakespeare. Inside it is "argent", which means silver in heraldry and money in French. Conflicts: Marg ERP (near-spelling; accounting and billing ERP) (Accounting / billing software (India)); Margn (near-spelling; Capterra UK listing) (Software); Margento (near-spelling) (Unclear from results).

**Yield** (YEELD). The return produced by an asset. "Yield management" is the pricing discipline airlines and hotels use to get the most revenue from every seat or room. Conflicts: Yield App (Crypto and digital wealth fintech); Yield Book (Fixed-income analytics software); Microsoft Xandr Yield Analytics (Ad-tech analytics product); DR YIELD Software & Solutions GmbH (Semiconductor yield software).

**Tickmark** (TICK-mark). The small mark an auditor puts beside a figure once it has been checked against evidence. Conflicts: Tickmark - audit/accounting workpaper software (listed on Capterra, G2, GetApp) (Accounting / audit software); Tickmark Solutions, Inc. (trademarks) (Software); Tickmark, Inc. (trademarks) (Software); Tickmark Solutions (PitchBook profile) (Software).

**Parity** (PAIR-ih-tee). Equality between two things. In IT, parity (a parity bit, or RAID parity) is the mechanism that detects lost or corrupted data and rebuilds it. Conflicts: ParityDeals (No-code billing and pricing platform for SaaS); Parity Software (Software (Gartner-reviewed product)); Parity (Canada) (HVAC optimisation software, Series B); parity (Germany) (Custom software development).

**Remit** (rih-MIT). A real word with two business meanings. To remit is to send payment ("please remit within 30 days"). In British business English, your remit is the agreed scope of your responsibility ("that's outside our remit"). Conflicts: Remit (seller listed on G2) (Software); Remit (company profile on CB Insights) (Fintech/payments); Remitly (similar name) (Fintech, remittances); NetRemit (Remittance software).

**Keel** (KEEL). The structural spine along the bottom of a ship that keeps it stable and stops it drifting sideways. "On an even keel" means steady and balanced. Conflicts: Keel (UK BaaS fintech infrastructure, formerly neobank Frost, exited stealth May 2026) (Fintech / banking-as-a-service); Keel (funded startup, seed 2024) (Startup (various)); Keel Infrastructure (TSX: KEEL) (Listed company, software category on Simply Wall St).

**Ingot** (ING-gut). A cast bar of refined precious metal: raw material turned into stored value. Conflicts: INGOT Brokers - online forex/CFD broker (Fintech / trading); Ingot Coin (Crypto); Ingot AI (PitchBook profile) (AI software).

**Ambit** (AM-bit). The scope, extent or bounds of something ("within the ambit of the agreement"). From the Latin ambitus, a going around or a boundary. Conflicts: Ambit Software (Software / SugarCRM partner); Ambit AI (AI software startup); Ambit Analytics Inc. (Analytics); Ambit Design Systems (Electronic design software).

**TrueUp** (TROO-up). From "true-up", the reconciliation that adjusts what is billed to what is actually used. MSPs know it from Microsoft licensing true-ups and from "truing up" a client's agreement to their current seat and device count. Conflicts: TrueUp (trueup.io) (Tech jobs and company-tracking platform).

**Compound** (KOM-pownd). Compound growth: small recurring amounts that build on themselves over time. Conflicts: Compound Finance (DeFi lending protocol); Compound (wealth management firm) (Fintech, wealth advisory).

**Worth** (WURTH). The value of something, and what it deserves. Conflicts: Worth (Worth AI) - underwriting/onboarding automation for financial services ($30M Series A, 2026) (Fintech SaaS); Worth - $25M raise, coverage in FinTech Futures (Fintech SaaS); Worth AI company profile (Fintech).

**Coffer** (KOF-er). A strongbox for money. 'The coffers' are an organisation's funds. Conflicts: CloudCoffer (Network security); Coffer - subscription tracker app (Product Hunt) (Personal finance app); Coffer - crypto portfolio app (Consumer crypto/finance app); Coffers - cashback deals app (Consumer finance app).
