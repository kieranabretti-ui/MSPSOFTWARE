# Product lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The one sentence exists.** "Find the work your MSP is doing for free" is clear, concrete and a promise the buyer can check. Most early products cannot say what they are in one line. This one can.
- **The output is built with care.** Every opportunity shows its evidence, how its value was calculated, a confidence level with the reason, and a recommended action. That is the right shape for a sceptical ex-technician owner: it shows its working instead of asking for trust.
- **It is honest by design.** Figures are framed as "potential leakage to review", and the security page says plainly what is not in place yet. Refusing to overclaim is a taste decision, and it is the right one for this buyer.
- **The workflow ends in action.** New, Reviewing, Approved, Actioned turns a report into work the owner can get done. That is what separates a product from a dashboard.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The first five minutes is a data chore, and it has never been tested.** The experience starts with a busy owner exporting tickets, time, users and devices, billing and contract PDFs from several systems, with no PSA export guides, no integrations and no guided route. This is the biggest gap in the experience, and no audit has been run on a real MSP's data. If the uploads fail or the findings look wrong on real exports, the owner never reaches the evidence screen where the product is good. Nobody knows how long it takes from sign-up to the first credible finding on real data.
2. **Five detectors plus a category ambition is not focus.** The product finds five kinds of leakage, and the plan grows into profitability, contract optimisation, pricing, forecasting and automated change orders. The lens asks what one thing it must do brilliantly. The demo does not settle it: four categories each come in around £700 to £1,250. Spreading effort across five half-proven detectors, with a roadmap of seven more, is how the core gets mediocre.
3. **The trust touchpoints are missing, and this product asks for the most sensitive data an MSP holds.** There is no privacy policy, no terms, no DPA, no MFA and no multi-user workspace, and the live site runs in browser-only mode. The buyer sees all of these seams before seeing any value. A small craft lapse feeds the same doubt: the headline £4,281 includes £336 counted twice. It is disclosed, but a product built on "every pound has its evidence" should never double-count a pound in its own headline.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- If you could keep only one of the five detectors, which would it be, and why does the owner care about that one most?
- Walk me through a real owner's first five minutes. Which PSA do they export from, which files, and how many clicks before the first finding? (This has not been measured. Run it with real owners. /founder-consumer covers the buyer side.)
- How many findings on real data would an owner mark as wrong? There is no precision figure, because there is no real audit yet.
- Who handles the audit when the self-serve upload fails? Where is the hand-off between your software, the owner's PSA and you, and what breaks there?
- Do the leakage figures found in a typical real MSP justify £249 a month? The demo is fictional. Real-world leakage per MSP is an open question for /founder-cfo, and what competing tools already show is one for /founder-competitors.
- Why should the headline include any overlap at all?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pick the one thing.** Lead with one or two detectors (the founder chooses, ideally from what owners say hurts most) and park the "commercial intelligence" roadmap until the core is proven on real data.
2. **Make the first five minutes work end to end.** Write export guides for the most common UK PSAs, offer a guided or done-with-you audit route, and run at least 3 to 5 real-MSP audits. Measure time to first credible finding and the share of findings owners accept.
3. **Close the trust seams before asking for real data.** Publish a privacy policy, terms and a DPA, add MFA, and turn on the backend.
4. **Fix the headline maths.** Remove overlaps from the headline total so that every pound is counted once.
