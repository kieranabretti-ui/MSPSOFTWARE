## 2. What Headroom does {#service}

Headroom analyses data you export from your own systems (clients, tickets, time entries, users and devices, billing lines, and contract documents) and highlights possible commercial opportunities: work that may be unbilled, out of scope or underpriced, agreement drift, and similar. Each opportunity shows its evidence, calculation and a confidence level. You can track opportunities through to a decision and export reports.

Headroom works from files you upload. It does not connect to your PSA, RMM or accounting system. Features marked as "Planned" on our pricing page are not part of the service until we release them.

## 9. AI explanations {#ai}

When you ask for one, Headroom can generate an AI explanation of a single opportunity using Anthropic's Claude model. AI output can be wrong or incomplete. We check that quotes shown under an explanation appear word for word in your evidence and reject explanations that introduce figures not in your data, but the explanation itself may still be inaccurate. Treat it as a starting point, and check it against the evidence before relying on it. AI never produces or changes the figures Headroom shows. Using this feature sends that opportunity and its evidence to Anthropic, as described in our [privacy policy](/privacy#ai).
